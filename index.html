About the Studio

Founded in 2026, Aetheria Interactive is an independent game studio dedicated to creating high-quality, high-performance gaming experiences. We build original worlds, custom game architectures, and unique mechanics for players worldwide.



Aetheria:
- Greek Root: The name comes from the ancient Greek word αἰθήρ (aither), which means the bright, luminous upper atmosphere or the pure air breathed by the gods.

- Mythology: In Greek mythology, Aither was the primordial deity of light and the upper sky. Aetheria is a feminized form carrying the sense of celestial purity, radiance, and soft, fragile beauty.
